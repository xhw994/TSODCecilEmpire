## Overview

This mod adds the **Cecil Empire** civilization to Civilization VI, led by **Gawain Cecil**. Inspired by the web novel *Sword of Dawn*, the civilization is built around great person recruitment, scientific advancement, and strategic district planning. It suits players who enjoy snowballing through technology while maintaining a credible military threat.

It is one of the mods of the *Sword of Dawn* series, with the Typhon Empire and Dream City mods. The whole series is in the [Sword of Dawn series collection](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3829784925) on the Steam Workshop: subscribe to all of the mods with one click.

## Civilization Features

### Civilization Ability: *Quantifying the Truth*

- Quarries provide **+1 Science**.
- Mined Niter provides **+3 Faith**.
- The first time each specialty district or Campus building is completed, receive a **unique Great Person of Cecil** (13 in all; full list in [README_EN.md](README_EN.md)).

---

### Unique Unit: Gorgon Platform

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Replaces | Drone (support unit) |
| Range bonus to siege units within 1 tile | +1 Range |
| Bombard bonus to siege units within 1 tile | +5 Bombard Strength |
| Passive | Heals adjacent units each turn |

The Gorgon Platform supercharges siege warfare. Position it behind your frontline to amplify bombardment and keep units healthy during prolonged campaigns.

---

### Unique Building: Mana Obelisk

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Replaces | Monument |
| Unlock | Mathematics |
| Yields | +2 Science, +2 Production, +1 Amenity |
| Adjacency | All districts receive an additional standard adjacency bonus |
| Special | After researching **Electricity**, all buildings receive **doubled base yields** |

The Obelisk delays your early Monument but pays off dramatically in the mid-to-late game. Plan your city layout for maximum district adjacency from the start.

---

### Leader: Gawain Cecil

#### Ability: *The Sword of Dawn*

- Recruiting a great person grants a **+20% yield bonus** in that great person's category.
- Each additional recruit of the **same type** adds another **+10%**, up to a **+100% cap**.
- **All** recruited great people provide a **+5% bonus** to every great person point yield.

The ability rewards committing to specific great person types early and stacking recruits of the same category for exponential returns.

#### Agenda: *Truth Seeker*

- **Likes** civilizations with high Science output.
- **Dislikes** civilizations neglecting Science.

## Recommended Victory Paths

| Victory Type | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **Science** | Rush districts → recruit Great Scientists → leverage Obelisk's doubled yields post-Electricity |
| **Domination** | Stack Great Generals + Gorgon Platforms for siege-focused armies; use Science lead for tech advantages |

**Early game tip:** Compensate for the delayed Monument (Obelisk requires Mathematics) by securing an early **Pantheon** with a faith-boosting belief, and settle near quarries or saltpeter for the civilization ability bonuses.

## Installation

### Steam Workshop *(recommended)*

1. Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3251898146).
2. Launch Civilization VI → **Additional Content** → enable **Cecil Empire**.

### Manual Installation

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Copy the mod folder to your Civ VI mods directory:
   - **Windows:** `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Mods\`
   - **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/Sid Meier's Civilization VI/Mods/`
3. Launch Civilization VI → **Additional Content** → enable **Cecil Empire**.

## Compatibility

Tested against approximately **150 community mods** with no major conflicts detected. Mods that significantly alter:

- Monument yields or replacement buildings
- Drone unit behavior
- Great person point generation formulas

…may interact unexpectedly. Please open an issue if you find a conflict.
